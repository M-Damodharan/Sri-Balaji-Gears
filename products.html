PRIMARY AUTOMOTIVE SEATING DIVISION · UNO MINDA PARTNER

Two-Seat Bus Passenger Seat Assemblies

Our flagship high-volume 2-passenger seating frame assemblies engineered for state transport corporations, intercity luxury coaches, and commercial buses in partnership with Tier-1 seating suppliers like UNO MINDA.

  • Structural Frame: Cold-drawn ERW steel tubes (Ø25.4mm & Ø31.8mm) with multi-radius CNC ergonomic bends.
  • Floor Mount Gussets: 3.0mm – 4.0mm high-tensile anchor plates with pre-drilled M10/M12 chassis bolt slots.
  • Welding Execution: Continuous robotic GMAW fillet welds at all primary tubular intersections.
  • Safety Compliance: Meets Indian AIS 023 seat anchor and dynamic passenger load specifications.

TVS EXPORT PACKING STRUCTURE & CHASSIS DIVISION

TVS Export Packing Structure

Heavy-duty TVS Export Packing Structures, tubular frameworks, and high-tensile reinforcement assemblies manufactured for TVS Motor Company.

  • Torsional Rigidity: Engineered for dynamic shipping shock absorption, secure transit, and cyclic fatigue endurance.
  • Fixture Precision: 100% pneumatic clamped jig welding guarantees coordinate repeatability < 0.5mm.
  • Deep Penetration Welds: Macro-etched weld joints guaranteeing complete root fusion and zero lack-of-fusion voids.
  • Direct Line-Side Ready: Fully pre-treated, primed, or powder-coated for immediate export transit packing integration.

Heavy Pressing & Stamped Brackets

High-tonnage mechanical and hydraulic press stamping lines delivering precision floor anchor brackets, chassis reinforcements, seat base dishes, and deep-drawn components for Tier-1 automotive leaders.

  • Tonnage Capability: 50T to 300T mechanical and hydraulic stamping press lines with pneumatic cushion systems.
  • Material Thickness: Heavy-gauge blanking, piercing, and progressive forming from 1.2mm up to 6.0mm high-tensile steel.
  • Tooling Precision: In-house tool & die maintenance guaranteeing sharp cutting edges, zero burrs, and minimal die wear.
  • Quality Verification: 100% coordinate inspection on dedicated checking fixtures ensuring perfect assembly hole pitch.

CERTIFIED MANUAL & ROBOTIC WELDING

Precision Fabrication Assemblies

Dedicated fabrication cells combining certified manual CO2/MIG/TIG welding with multi-axis tubular bending to produce complex structural assemblies, cross-members, and custom commercial vehicle frameworks.

  • Tubular Bending: Multi-radius CNC cold tube bending with zero cross-sectional distortion or flattening.
  • Certified Welders: Welders certified under automotive Tier-1 standards for structural load integrity.
  • Rigid Fixturing: Custom pneumatic clamping fixtures maintaining coordinate repeatability within ±0.5mm.
  • Surface Pre-Treatment: Multi-stage degreasing, shot blasting, and protective primer ready for line assembly.
