PRIMARY AUTOMOTIVE SEATING DIVISION

Three-Seat Bus Seating Assembly

Our flagship high-volume seating frame assembly designed for state transport corporations and private coach builders via Tier-1 seating suppliers like UNO MINDA.

  • Structural Frame: Cold-drawn ERW steel tubes (Ø25.4mm & Ø31.8mm) with 1.6mm – 2.0mm wall thickness.
  • Floor Mount Gussets: 3.0mm – 4.0mm high-tensile anchor plates with pre-drilled M10/M12 chassis bolt slots.
  • Welding Execution: Continuous robotic GMAW fillet welds at all primary tubular intersections.
  • Safety Compliance: Meets Indian AIS 023 seat anchor and dynamic passenger load specifications.

Two-Seat Luxury Passenger Frames

Engineered for intercity luxury coaches and staff transport buses, incorporating integrated brackets for mechanical recliners, adjustable footrests, and reinforced grab handles.

  • Ergonomic Contour: Multi-radius CNC pipe bends delivering anatomical lumbar support.
  • Reclining Mechanism Mounts: Laser-pierced bracket mounts aligned for seamless mechanical actuator fitment.
  • Armrest & Seat Belt Fixations: Built-in 2-point and 3-point seatbelt anchoring gussets tested for pull strength.
  • Vibration Dampening: Rigid triangulated crossbars prevent harmonic road flutter.

AUTOMOTIVE CHASSIS & FRAME DIVISION

Auto Body Chassis Tubular Frame Parts

Tubular subframes, cross-members, engine mount cradles, and suspension gusset brackets manufactured for major OEMs including TVS Motor Company.

  • Torsional Rigidity: Engineered for high dynamic shock absorption and cyclic fatigue endurance.
  • Fixture Precision: 100% pneumatic clamped jig welding guarantees coordinate repeatability < 0.5mm.
  • Deep Penetration Welds: Macro-etched weld joints guaranteeing complete root fusion and zero lack-of-fusion voids.
  • Direct Line-Side Ready: Fully pre-treated, primed, or powder-coated for immediate assembly line integration.

Pressed Brackets & Heavy Stampings

Precision pressed automotive stampings, suspension reinforcements, floor anchor gussets, and battery tray supports produced on our mechanical and hydraulic press fleet.

  • Thickness Range: Sheet metal blanking and forming from 1.2mm up to 6.0mm high-tensile steel.
  • In-House Tool Maintenance: Tool room technicians maintaining progressive dies with quick changeovers.
  • Burr-Free Finishes: Edge chamfering and deburring to eliminate sharp edges prior to welding or coating.
