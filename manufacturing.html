Production Systems & Welding Cells

PRECISION ENGINEERING
FROM RAW STEEL TO ASSEMBLY.

Our integrated 50,000 sq. ft. manufacturing floor in SIPCOT Hosur combines CNC tube bending, 50T–250T mechanical and hydraulic pressing, dedicated robotic welding cells, and certified manual MIG/TIG stations to deliver reliable automotive structures.

End-to-End Workflow

INTEGRATED PRODUCTION FLOW
IN 6 RIGOROUS STEPS.

Every automotive welded assembly moves through systematic stages with strict stage-gate quality verification to ensure zero defects and dimensional stability.

01

Raw Material Gating

IQC inspection of ERW steel tubes, seamless pipes, and CRCA sheet coils against chemical and mechanical test mill certificates.

02

CNC Bending & Cutting

Automated cold circular sawing and multi-axis CNC tube bending for ergonomic seat frame profiles with zero ovality deformation.

03

Pressing & Stamping

50T to 250T mechanical and hydraulic press fleet producing heavy-gauge mounting brackets, floor anchor plates, and chassis gussets.

04

Welding (Robotic & Manual)

6-axis robotic welding cells for high-speed continuous seams and AWS-qualified manual welders for complex structural joints.

05

Inspection & Fixture Check

100% verification on custom pneumatic Go/No-Go inspection fixtures, weld throat measurement, and dye-penetrant crack testing.

06

Finishing & Line Delivery

Anti-corrosion 7-tank phosphate pre-treatment, powder coating, protective crating, and scheduled daily Kanban replenishment directly to OEM assembly lines.
