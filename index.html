Featured Assembly · Seating Systems

BUS SEAT FRAMES
WELDED FOR STRENGTH.

Engineered for high-volume commercial vehicle passenger comfort and crash-worthiness. Our 3-seat bus seating frames are manufactured using precision tubular bending, dedicated pneumatic fixtures, and certified continuous weld beads.

  • ERW Precision Steel Tubes: High yield strength, uniform wall thickness, zero ovality distortion.
  • Robust Floor Anchor Plates: Heavy-gauge mounting brackets designed for dynamic passenger shear loads.
  • Multi-point Welded Joints: Full penetration fillet welds tested for vibration fatigue and peel strength.
  • Anti-Corrosion Surface Finish: 7-tank pre-treatment and durable thermoset powder coating.
