Who We Are

WELDING SOLUTIONS

Sri Balaji Gears undertakes bus seat welding, tubular carrier fabrication, and auto body frame welding work. Our company profile is centred on durable fabricated structures and precision welded components for automotive applications.

We support demanding OEM and Tier-1 production requirements through clear coordination, strict fixture discipline, CO₂ welding quality, and attention to assembly fit-up. Our digital portal also provides interactive 3D sample previews so customers can inspect our workmanship online from any device.

  • Responsive support for prototype samples and production batches
