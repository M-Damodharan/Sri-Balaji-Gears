Enterprise Profile & Legacy

OVER 25 YEARS OF
MANUFACTURING EXCELLENCE.

Founded in 1999 in SIPCOT Industrial Area, Hosur, Sri Balaji Gears has evolved into a key automotive fabrication partner, supplying high-volume bus seating frames and chassis assemblies with 400 dedicated professionals across a 70,000 sq. ft. campus.

Our Background & Scale

DRIVEN BY METALLURGICAL
DISCIPLINE & TRUST.

What started as a precision press component shop has scaled into an integrated automotive fabrication powerhouse. Today, Sri Balaji Gears operates dedicated welding lines for commercial vehicle seating systems and automotive frame assemblies.

  • Established 1999: 25+ continuous years of Tier-1 OEM manufacturing experience.
  • SIPCOT Phase I, Hosur: Located inside the premier automotive industrial hub with immediate access to Bangalore and Chennai corridors.
  • 400-Strong Workforce: Certified welders, tooling designers, press toolmakers, and QA metrology specialists.
  • 70,000 Sq. Ft. Land Area: Approx. 50,000 sq. ft. of heavy industrial built-up shopfloor.
  • Core OEM Specialization: Dedicated high-volume production for bus passenger seat frame assemblies and automotive chassis welded structures.

Evolution & Milestones

OUR JOURNEY OF
ENGINEERING EXPANSION.

Key milestones that have shaped Sri Balaji Gears into a reliable Tier-1 fabrication partner for the Indian and global automotive sectors.

1999

Foundation in Hosur

Incorporated in SIPCOT Phase I, Hosur. Started operations with mechanical press lines and custom automotive component stamping.

2006

Fabrication & Welding Expansion

Expanded into precision tubular bending and structural frame welding to serve expanding commercial vehicle demands.

2012

Dedicated Seating Frame Lines

Established dedicated bus passenger seat welding cells, achieving high-volume supply contracts with Tier-1 seating suppliers like UNO Minda.

2018+

Robotic Automation & Scale

Integrated 6-axis robotic welding cells, upgraded metrology, and expanded to 400 employees across a 70,000 sq. ft. facility.

Core Values

THE PILLARS OF OUR
PRODUCTION PHILOSOPHY.

Every welded joint, pressed bracket, and assembled seat frame is produced under rigorous operational guidelines.

🎯

Zero-Defect Quality

Strict In-Process Quality Checks (IPQC) and dedicated Go/No-Go inspection fixtures for 100% fit-up guarantee before dispatch.

⚡

Direct-on-Line Supply Reliability

Direct-on-Line (DOL) capability with daily scheduled Kanban logistics serving OEM assembly plants across South India with zero production line stoppages.

🛡️

Operator Safety & 5S

Comprehensive shopfloor ergonomics, fume extraction systems, personal protective equipment (PPE), and continuous 5S housekeeping.

🔬

Metallurgical Integrity

Verified raw material mill test certificates, macro-etch weld penetration tests, and tensile shear evaluations for critical safety joints.
